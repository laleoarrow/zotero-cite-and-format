# Research Manuscripts

Use this reference for original research articles, clinical studies, omics studies, methods papers, and research-style major sections inside Word manuscripts.

## Core Principle
Research manuscripts need section discipline before they need polish.

## Section Role Gate
- Introduction:
  - move from problem to gap to study aim
  - keep background proportional
  - do not let it read like the Discussion
- Methods:
  - preserve operational detail, software/version/reporting statements, and hierarchy rules
  - keep design caveats here when they are about the current study
- Results:
  - report findings objectively
  - preserve adjusted versus unadjusted labels, effect sizes, units, denominators, and figure/table references
  - do not import interpretation that belongs in the Discussion
- Discussion:
  - start with the answer to the scientific question
  - distinguish confirmation, extension, and uncertainty
  - keep limitations explicit and proportionate

## Research Prose Filters
- Do not rewrite Results into review-style synthesis.
- Do not turn a Methods paragraph into a vague summary if the detail is needed for reproducibility.
- Do not inflate novelty in titles, abstracts, or discussion openings.
- Keep claims matched to design strength. Observational and association data do not justify causal wording by default.

## Reporting and Formatting Gates
- Preserve effect sizes, confidence intervals, units, sample counts, and model labels exactly unless the authoritative source changes.
- Keep statistical `P` formatting consistent with `sci-formatting.md`.
- When journal policy is silent, prefer conventional SCI reporting over local habit or memory.
- If the article type has structured abstract labels or reporting checklist language, preserve the exact order and wording required by the journal.

## Interaction with Common Formatting
- `manuscript-formatting.md` remains the common Word-formatting base.
- This file adds research-manuscript section and reporting gates on top of that base.
- Do not load the review-manuscript branch for a research-paper task unless the user is editing a genuine review-style section or companion document.
